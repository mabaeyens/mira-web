What ties them together

Made the same way, on purpose.

Different jobs, one set of rules. Whichever you open, it behaves the way I'd want software on my own devices to behave.

01

On your device

The work happens on the device in your hand, and I don't run a server. I couldn't read your data even if I wanted to.

02

No accounts

No sign-up screen, no login, no email asked for. Download, open, use.

03

Open where it counts

Mira's engine, Vera and the Pebble watchfaces are MIT-licensed. Don't take my word for what they do; read the code.

04

Built in the open

One person and Claude Code, shipping a small release most weeks. The commit history is public, warts included.
