Aura · iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Android

Spain's weather, on every screen you own.

Aura reads AEMET's open data (Spain's national weather service) through its public OpenData API and your own free key, and presents it clearly on every screen you own: widgets, Apple Watch complications, real observed temperatures, and avisos matched to your town.

Now on Android too, built natively in Kotlin, with its own home-screen widget. No backend, no account, no telemetry: the device is the hub.

The same screen, dawn to storm to night. The sky follows the real sun.

What it does

An editorial Hoy screen

Hoy opens on a hero written on-device in plain Spanish, then a full card stack: current conditions, the hourly strip, a seven-day outlook, a sunrise to sunset arc, wind, air quality (MITECO ICA), the UV index, the nearest radar, and the official AEMET bulletin, all over a live sun-tracking sky. Predicción keeps AEMET's human-written narrative for your community, resolved to the one that covers today.

Widgets and Lock Screen

Home Screen (small, medium, large, plus the iPad extra-large) over the same living sky, and Lock Screen (circular, rectangular, inline) families, each configurable to a specific saved location via App Intents. Every card renders from the same shared code as the app, so nothing drifts.

Apple Watch, live

A watch app and a face complication (corner, circular, rectangular, inline), fed from the phone over WatchConnectivity. The screen and the complication both update in place as the phone syncs, no separate fetch, no separate key.

Real readings and warnings

The observed temperature from the nearest AEMET station, not just the forecast. And avisos, the official CAP weather warnings, fetched, unpacked on-device, and matched to each saved location by province.

Your places, your order

Favourites from a bundled list of all 8,122 Spanish municipalities AEMET covers, or the nearest city to your current GPS location. Reorder, delete, pick the active one. The favourites list is mirrored to the widgets, so their picker lists exactly the places you saved.

Private by design

Your AEMET key lives in the device Keychain, or Android's encrypted storage, never in the binary, the repo, or a server. There is no backend: the app caches each location's snapshot on the device, and the widgets and Apple Watch read it from there. Nothing leaves your devices.

Now on Android

A native Android port, phone-first, in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose: the same editorial Hoy screen, the living sun-tracking sky, air quality, UV, wind and avisos, plus a home-screen widget you can pin to any saved place. No Google Play Services, no analytics, coarse location only. Coming to Google Play. The source is on GitHub.

Aura on Pebble

Aura is the name I use for everything I build for the Pebble Time 2, because it all started under the weather umbrella: Aura Essential and Aura LCD watchfaces, and Aura Sky Weather and Aura Cityscape Weather, the weather as watchapps. All free and open source. See them on the Pebble page.
