Spain's weather, on every Apple surface.

Aura reads AEMET — Spain's national weather service — through the OpenData API and your own free key, then puts it where the official app never did: home-screen and Lock Screen widgets, Apple Watch complications, real observed temperatures, and avisos matched to your town. No backend, no account, no telemetry — the device is the hub.

What it does

The official data, on the surfaces the official app forgot.

Two views, straight from AEMET

Hoy is the numeric daily forecast — min/max temperature, humidity, on-device sunrise and sunset — for your municipality. Predicción is the official, human-written narrative bulletin AEMET issues for your autonomous community, resolved to the one that covers today.

Widgets & Lock Screen

Home-screen (small / medium / large) and Lock Screen (circular / rectangular / inline) families, each configurable to a specific saved location via App Intents. Every card renders from the same shared code as the app, so nothing drifts.

Apple Watch, live

A watch app and a face complication (corner / circular / rectangular / inline), fed from the phone over WatchConnectivity. The screen and the complication both update in place as the phone syncs — no separate fetch, no separate key.

Real readings & warnings

The observed temperature from the nearest AEMET station, not just the forecast. And avisos — the official CAP weather warnings — fetched, unpacked on-device, and matched to each saved location by province.

Your places, your order

Favourites from a bundled list of every provincial capital, or the nearest city to your current GPS location. Reorder, delete, pick the active one. The favourites list is mirrored to the widgets, so their picker lists exactly the places you saved.

Private by design

Your AEMET key lives in the device Keychain — never in the binary, the repo, or a server. There is no backend: the app caches each location's snapshot to an App Group that the widgets and Watch read. Nothing leaves your devices. Adaptive dark and light.
