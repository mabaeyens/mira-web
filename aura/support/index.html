Step by step

Adding your AEMET key

There are two moments to do this: the first time you open Aura (the onboarding shows you the screen automatically), or any time afterwards from Settings. The numbers on each screenshot mark exactly where to tap. These screenshots are from iPhone; on Android it's the same idea, open Settings (the gear, top right) and paste your key under the AEMET key field.

The first time you open Aura

12
  1. Tap Solicitar mi clave gratis. AEMET's site opens; sign up and they email you a free key. (Prefer to do it later? Pasar, top right, skips this, you can always come back from Settings.)

Questions

Frequently asked

Where does the data come from?

From official sources: AEMET, Spain's national weather service, for the forecast, the warnings (avisos) and the UV index, and MITECO for air quality.

Do I need an account or a sign-up?

No. Aura works with no account, no sign-up and no ads. It collects no personal data.

Do I need an AEMET key?

Yes, and it is free. Aura reads AEMET OpenData through your own key. Request one for free at opendata.aemet.es, then paste it into Settings (Ajustes). It takes a minute, and the app stores the key only on your device.

Can I use Aura without sharing my location?

Yes. You can choose your municipality by hand. If you grant location access, the place is matched on your device and only the municipality's public code ever leaves it.

Why does the UV index say "daily max"?

AEMET publishes UV as a clear-sky daily maximum, not hour by hour. That is why Aura labels it as the day's maximum rather than the value right now.

Does it work on iPad and Apple Watch?

Yes, with widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen, and a complication on the Apple Watch.

Is Aura on Android?

Yes, a native phone app, built in Kotlin, with a Home-screen widget. It's coming to Google Play; in the meantime the source is on GitHub. The iPad and Apple Watch extras are iOS-only; on Android it's the phone and its widget.

Do tips unlock anything?

No. Tips are an optional way to say thank you and unlock no features. Aura is just as complete without them.

How is my privacy handled?

Aura has no servers of mine and collects no personal data. You can read the full privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

There's nothing on a server to delete, Aura keeps no copy of your data off your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes the only copy. There's more detail in the privacy policy.
