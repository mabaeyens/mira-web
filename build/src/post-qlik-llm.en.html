Many on-prem customers ask me the same thing: "When does AI come to Qlik Sense on Windows?"

Mar 2026 · First published on LinkedIn, in Spanish

The short answer: it is not coming. Not like in Cloud. The compute capacity it requires is impossible to reproduce on-prem.

But I understand the frustration. And instead of just saying it, I got to work.

In early 2024 I spent several months exploring ways to integrate LLMs into Qlik Sense, without depending on the cloud, with local models or any OpenAI-compatible API:

  • A visualization extension that analyses in natural language the data of the chart you have on screen
  • LLM built directly into Qlik expressions and load scripts
  • A RAG pipeline to query internal PDF documents

It is a lot of work: it requires programming, maintenance, AI knowledge, and even so it does not come close to what Qlik Cloud offers out of the box, Qlik Answers among other capabilities.

I am not a software engineer. I am a solutions architect. The goal was not the code, it was to understand whether it is possible and how. It is, and it works.

That exercise, which was possible thanks to the patience and guidance of José Díaz López, convinced me more than anything else: it is not that Cloud is better.

It is that on-prem cannot compete in this area.

Today I decided to publish the result of this effort, after several weeks catching up on Qlik Cloud's capabilities and the February 10 announcements. Better late than never.
