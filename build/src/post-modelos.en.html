The models are not the problem

May 2026 · First published on LinkedIn, in Spanish

I have spent weeks building a local agent and several tools to help me code and with daily tasks. The model reasons, generates code, keeps context. But it does not search the web, does not calculate, does not connect to my tools on its own. That is what I build.

In that time, five new top-tier open-weight models have come out. Two big providers have announced investments of tens of billions in infrastructure. The bottleneck in AI is no longer the model's intelligence, it is the compute behind it, and there is not enough for everyone.

The noise is constant. Every launch brings its benchmarks, its comparisons, its opinion threads from people who have built nothing with any of them. Gurus without scars.

It is impossible to follow everything. Trying paralyses you. Building moves you forward.

What you learn by building is not the numbers in those comparisons: it is that the model is the starting point, not the destination. What decides whether something works is what surrounds it. The tools you connect to it, the instructions you give it, the context you prepare for your specific case. That ecosystem is not transferable.

There is no ecosystem that is good for everyone: there is yours.

The model is the environment variable. The ecosystem is the program.
