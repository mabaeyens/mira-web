I am programming again after 18 years

Apr 2026 · First published on LinkedIn, in Spanish

My first program was in BASIC, in the late 80s, saved on cassette tapes. After that came Perl, CGI, web pages written by hand on UNIX servers, the terminal as the only environment. Then came other jobs, with little time to put hours in and little desire to start on new languages.

So many things to learn, programming got left behind.

It is not that I did not want to come back. It is that there was always something else, and when I looked at the new ecosystems, the frameworks, the tools, I did not know where to start. That went on for years, until Claude Code arrived.

What changed was not the time or the desire. It was being able to move forward without having everything under control.

Now I have someone to think through the complex decisions with. Someone to sort out the syntax when I get stuck. And I learn too.

In early 2024 I finished my first real project, which I documented in an earlier post.

And I have kept going since. Most of the time I put into it is inconsistent, a few weekends with very intense sessions. Right now I am building an agent to help me code locally with Gemma 4 and Ollama, no external APIs and no worrying about tokens. Two repos: one public, one not. A Mac app. An iOS app. Nobody asked me for it.

If you had told me two years ago, I would not have believed it.

Eighteen years without programming. And now with the same excitement as when the program ran for the first time and the screen did something you had asked it to.

That excitement does not change.
